COORDINATES AND RELATIONSHIPS
X is the main longitudinal axis, with rear flange outside face X=0 and
front flange outside face X=142. Y is transverse and Z is up. The main
axis is Y=Z=0. Rear flange is X=0..10; cylinder ends at X=80; cone ends
at X=130; front flange is X=130..142. Tower center is X=45, Y=0.
Stand is built in position: X=0..130, Y=-35..35, Z=-80..0.
Its base rear edge aligns with the housing rear face. Saddle plate is
X=120..130. Base is 10 thick. Central rib is X=30..100, width 15.
The saddle and rib are Boolean-trimmed by the same solid exterior
master used to construct the housing. Stand mounting holes use the
main front-flange lower bolt centers and diameter. These relationships
are regenerated by build.py, rather than independent stand dimensions.

   The bore is exactly 3 degrees, from diameter 45 at X=0 to diameter

   7 deep from the front.
2. The keyway is 5 wide, with its top at Z=25. Its axial extent is not
   explicitly dimensioned. It is interpreted as limited to the 7 mm
   counterbore depth, ending at that shoulder.
3. Section B-B on step 3 shows a blind hole on each side of the barrel.
   Both are modeled at X=45, Z=0, diameter 8.5, full-diameter depth 5.
   Conical drill tips are visible on that section but their angle is not
   specified; a conventional 118 degree included tip is used beyond the
   5 mm cylindrical depth.

   distances. Plates are 20 along X and 10 tangentially; each diameter
   12 bore crosses the 10 mm plate thickness. Lugs are at +/-45 degrees

   intersection is X=105 (37 from front face; 25 from cone end). The
